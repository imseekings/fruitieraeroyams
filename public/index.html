Infrastructure impacts

Screening-level estimates from the mosaic’s temperature and rainfall change at one grid cell (about 300 km across), using simple rules of thumb: temperature distributions for heat, freeze–thaw and degree days, roughly 6.5% more intense rain per °C, about 0.7% line-capacity loss per °C, and scenario-based sea-level rise. They show where to look more closely and are not engineering assessments. Ranges show the spread across weighted models.

About the data. This page can’t download CMIP6 grids, so the fields are illustrative: ten model members built from idealized, well-documented patterns (Arctic amplification, faster land warming, a North Atlantic warming hole, wetter tropics and high latitudes, drier subtropics) with model-specific sensitivity, pattern noise and baseline biases. The “current climate” is a stylized stand-in for observations, and coastlines are coarse. Real model grids would drop into the same weighting and cross-referencing steps. Weights come from each model’s baseline error against the current climate, and projections use the delta method (current climate plus the mosaic change).
